# Product Launch Readiness Checklist

## Product & UX
- [ ] Primary user journeys tested and verified.
- [ ] Empty states, error messages, and loading spinners verified.

## Engineering & Operations
- [ ] End-to-end automated test suite passing.
- [ ] Production monitoring, error tracking, and alerting active.
- [ ] Database backup and rollback procedures verified.

## Legal & Compliance
- [ ] Privacy policy and Terms of Service linked in app.
- [ ] Cookie consent banner active where applicable.
